## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how **Penta Bot** collects, uses, and stores data when used in Discord.

## 2. Data We Collect

Penta Bot collects only the data required to provide its functionality:

### Discord Identifiers

- User IDs
- Server (Guild) IDs

### Game Data

- Match history and gameplay statistics

### User-Uploaded Images

- Images uploaded for bot customisation features

## 3. Data We Do NOT Collect

The Bot does **not** collect or store:

- Discord messages
- AI prompts
- AI-generated responses
- Private conversations
- Unrelated personal data
